How ShredDeals works

Read the price and discount together

The current price is the last sale price ShredDeals observed for that retailer listing. A retailer may advertise a sale or provide a comparison price. When a valid original or reference price is available, ShredDeals calculates the displayed percentage off from that reference and the current price. The reference may be a list price. Use the price history to see what ShredDeals actually observed shoppers being offered.

Use observed history as a separate reference

A price-history chart shows recorded prices for a listing. The lowest observed price is the lowest in the available record for that listing. The record covers the dates and offers ShredDeals saw. It can miss earlier sales, other stores, variants, and short-lived promotions. A recent drop compares tracked observations, so a lower price today can still sit above the tracked low. The Drop interval filter uses a decrease of at least $2 or 5% within the selected window.

Sort by discount or look at the deal score

The deal score combines discount depth, closeness to a tracked low, recent price drops, and a popular-size bonus where applicable. A high score says the listing did well on those price and size signals. Check fit, condition, shipping, and return terms before buying.

Compare offers across stores

Group matching products across stores brings together offers that ShredDeals identifies as likely matches. Retailer naming and specifications can differ. Some matches can be missed, and a group can contain offers that differ in size, variant, or included parts. Confirm those details on the retailer page.

Watch a listing

Stars and saved views stay in your browser. Email alerts, where enabled, require email confirmation. Delivery depends on a qualifying event being detected and an email being sent. Use the links in alert emails to manage alerts or unsubscribe.

Confirm price, inventory, coupon eligibility, and shipping at the retailer. See what to include when reporting a listing issue.
