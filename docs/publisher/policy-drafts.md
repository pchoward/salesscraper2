# Publisher policy drafts

**Owner review only.** Confirm the publisher identity, jurisdiction, contact and privacy request channel, effective dates, and actual service configuration before publication. The repository cannot establish every operational setting or legal obligation.

## Privacy policy draft

ShredDeals helps you browse action-sports offers. The site uses browser storage for favorites, saved views, interface preferences, and dismissed promotions. Clearing site data removes those local settings. Shareable page URLs can include search and filter selections, so review a URL before sharing it.

Catalog pages include Cloudflare Web Analytics to measure usage. Hosting, analytics, and security services may process technical request information, such as the requested page, device or browser information, and IP address. When you use email subscriptions or alerts, ShredDeals processes your email address, preferences, selected alert criteria, and subscription status to confirm and deliver messages. The alert implementation uses Cloudflare Workers and storage, Turnstile for abuse prevention, and Resend for email delivery in its production configuration. Use the links in alert emails to manage preferences or unsubscribe. The management interface also provides a deletion request flow.

Retailer links take you to independently operated sites. Their policies govern how they use information and handle purchases. Review their terms and privacy information before checkout.

**Owner to confirm** — Account and provider settings, data locations and retention, analytics and cookies in production, operational logs, legacy subscription flows, handling of requests and regional rights, policy contact, and whether any additional vendors or affiliate links process visitor data.

## Affiliate disclosure draft

ShredDeals may earn a commission from qualifying purchases through links labeled as affiliate links. Retailers set prices, shipping charges, coupon eligibility, and purchase terms. ShredDeals’ current deal score uses observed pricing, discounts, and size signals where available. The score has no commission input.

**Owner to confirm** — Actual participating merchants and networks, any effect of commercial agreements on editorial choices or placement, and checkout pricing implications. No AvantLink relationship is claimed. Publish a short plain-language disclosure before or alongside qualifying links when they exist, link to the full disclosure from the publisher navigation, and identify paid links with appropriate attributes. Check the actual consumer experience before switching on monetization. [FTC Endorsement Guides FAQ](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/ftcs-endorsement-guides-what-people-are-asking)

## Terms of use draft

ShredDeals provides informational listings and price observations. Prices, product details, stock, and coupon eligibility can change between scans. Confirm the offer at the retailer before ordering. Retailers handle orders, payment, shipping, returns, and applicable purchase terms.

Price charts reflect available observations and may omit promotions or offers. Grouped listings can include variants or incomplete matches. Alerts depend on observations and delivery and may arrive after an offer changes. Send listing corrections or site issues through the public contact channel once established.

**Owner to confirm** — Legal entity, jurisdiction, effective date, rights to use retailer content, and any applicable terms for conditions of use, disclaimers, or liability.
