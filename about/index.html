About ShredDeals

ShredDeals brings together sale listings from retailers that sell skateboarding, skiing, mountain biking, and BMX gear. Use it to compare offers, see how a listed price has moved, and decide whether a deal is worth a closer look.

Compare the gear you actually want

Start with a sport and category. Then narrow the catalog by retailer, brand, price, discount, or available specifications such as deck width. Quick View shows more about an offer, and the retailer’s site handles checkout, shipping, and returns.

Follow prices over time

ShredDeals records prices seen during catalog scans. When a listing has enough history, its chart and tracked low give you a second reference point alongside the price the retailer lists. You can star a listing in this browser. Where email alerts are enabled, choose what to watch, confirm your email, and manage or unsubscribe from alerts through the email links.

When listings update

Skate, ski, mountain bike, and BMX catalogs are scheduled to update daily, with backup runs if a scheduled scrape does not succeed. Publication can lag a scheduled scan, and retailers can change prices or stock between scans.
